## Current evidence

The manuscript derives a three-scalar matter action, a finite homogeneous radial branch, its local radial mass, and a conditional matter-sector null energy condition (NEC). It reports exploratory cylindrical linearized-gravity numbers after scaling a localized source by 10^-6. It explicitly leaves nonlinear Einstein–matter evolution and global non-collapse unestablished.

The repository implements the finite-branch algebra, the 2-by-2 kinetic-matrix positivity check, a sampled quadratic-form NEC diagnostic, and an integration routine for a supplied stationary cylindrical source. The geometry and evolution scripts are demonstrations. I cloned the public `main` branch and ran `python -m unittest discover -s tests -v`: all 10 tests passed. They cover the implemented algebra and a smooth-source grid-convergence example; this is not reproduction of the manuscript's numerical checkpoint.

## Review blockers, in priority order

1. **Reproduce the paper's reported numbers.** The repository contains no parameter set, field profiles, source generator, machine-readable outputs, or command that produces the reported largest metric perturbation `1.16 × 10^-3`, minimum `N_+ = 3.71 × 10^-14`, minimum `N_- = 1.69 × 10^-14`, and minimum energy density `2.62 × 10^-14`. Record the domain, grid, boundary data, dimensional conventions, scaling, and sign convention. Add residual and refinement tables for that exact case. Until then, label those values exploratory and unreplicated by the public code.
2. **Establish a self-consistent source and gauge.** Section VIII applies a Gaussian radial envelope to field-gradient source data as a modeling choice. Show whether the resulting `T_{μν}` obeys the matter equations and `∂^μ T_{μν}=0` to the claimed approximation. The harmonic-gauge linearized Einstein equation also imposes a conservation/gauge compatibility condition. Solving each radial component independently for an arbitrary supplied source does not establish it. Quantify the conservation and gauge residuals, or narrow the claim to a radial Poisson-type diagnostic.
3. **Separate physical claims from algebraic checks.** `nec_scan.py` evaluates `uᵀKu` for arbitrary two-component unit vectors. The manuscript's all-null-direction NEC statement follows conditionally from its action when `K` is positive semidefinite; the scan does not construct a full spacetime stress tensor or test every physical null vector. The finite branch and `m_eff² > 0` establish local homogeneous radial stability only. Keep that scope explicit in figures, abstract, and any review letter.
4. **Resolve identity and version metadata.** The repository lists ORCID `0009-0001-0672-7636`, whereas the 18-page manuscript lists `0009-0005-5931-2024`. Verify the correct identifier against the author's ORCID record before changing either. The manuscript PDF was uploaded in August but still states 21 July and calls itself a technical-conversion draft. Select one canonical review version, date it, and link its exact Git commit and DOI. The PDF's data statement says supplementary numerical data are included, while its author note says source and checks are future work; make both statements reflect the actual package.
5. **Complete the mathematical presentation.** Give a line-by-line variation and consistent index/sign conventions; restore dimensions/reference scales; spell out angular phase single-valuedness and winding, regular-axis and outer-boundary conditions, and why a local cylindrical model represents the proposed toroidal/helical setting. Check whether scaling `T` by `10^-6` is realized by a valid rescaling of fields/couplings or only by an imposed external source factor.
6. **Put related explorations in their proper place.** The repository does not reproduce the manuscript's earlier collapse toy model or prescribed off-diagonal metric searches. Either supply their scripts, parameters, outputs, and failure cases, or remove numerical specifics and describe them as motivation. Treat nonlinear constraint-satisfying initial data, trapped-surface diagnostics, and global behavior as a future test program.
7. **Editorial cleanup.** The manuscript has archival draft language, sparse references, and an AI-assistance/data statement that anticipates future submission. Audit every citation, replace provisional text, and prepare a clean APS/REVTeX manuscript only after the scientific package is stable.
